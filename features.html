Products Features

We current provide two types of solar panels of varying Watts as shown in the table below.

Monocrystalline Polycrystalline

Features:

High Efficiency Rating: Typically converts 20% to 24% of sunlight into electricity, outperforming polycrystalline and thin-film alternatives. Single-Crystal Silicon Composition: Made using the Czochralski process, which creates uniform silicon ingots. This enables electrons to flow more freely due to the lack of grain boundaries. Distinctive Appearance: Characterized by a uniform, dark black color and cells with cut-off corners (octagonal shapes) that leave small white gaps across the panel grid. Low Temperature Coefficient: Experiences smaller drops in energy production as ambient temperatures rise, performing better in hot climates than polycrystalline options. High Power-to-Space Ratio: Delivers greater electricity output per square foot, making them ideal for roof spaces with limited area. Long Lifespan: Offers a typical operational lifespan of 25 to 30+ years with low degradation rates over time.

Features:

Standard Efficiency Rating: Typically converts 15% to 18% of sunlight into electricity, providing a practical balance between performance and raw material utilization. Multi-Crystal Silicon Composition: Produced by melting multiple raw silicon fragments together into a block mold and slicing them into wafers. This creates internal grain boundaries that slightly restrict electron movement within the cell. Distinctive Appearance: Characterized by a speckled, mosaic-like blue hue caused by sunlight reflecting off the multiple individual silicon crystals, formed in square or rectangular cells. Moderate Temperature Coefficient: Operates with a temperature coefficient around -0.40%/°C to -0.45%/°C, resulting in standard output reductions as ambient temperatures rise in hot climates. Spatial Footprint Requirements: Generates moderate electricity output per square foot, requiring more installation area to achieve the total system capacity of higher-density options.Long Lifespan: Offers an operational lifespan of 25 to 30 years with standard annual performance degradation rates.
